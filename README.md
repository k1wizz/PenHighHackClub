## Pen High's (official?) Hack Club page!

This page introduces us to teenagers who are interested in joining our hack club branch/club or just want to take a look! The site explains who we are, what we offer to teach, what Hack Club is and a link to signup to the club. We have a contact page for people to reach out to us if they have any questions and a page full of example projects made by current and previous members for students to take inspiration from!

The projects page is still being worked on at the moment as I am still getting images to upload. But hopefully it will be completed before club day comes.
