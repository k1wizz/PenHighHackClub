Who are we?

Pen High Hack Club is a beginner friendly STEM based club helping teens learn how to make cool things! We teach students how to code, design, and build amazing projects using their creativity and imagination. Our club specializes in website development, CAD/3D modeling and PCBs (printed circuit boards).

What is Hack Club?

Hack Club is a non-profit organization. They offer tons of in person and online YSWS (You ship, we ship) programs and host hackathons/game jams around the world! In July 2026, they hosted a 7 day hackathon in Shenzhen, China!

Their mission is to help teens learn how to create things whenever and wherever they want. With hundreds of clubs and over 100,000 members around the world, Hack Club is changing the world by inspiring one teenager at a time.

For more information, visit their website: hackclub.com
